1. App Features

Wallpapers are included in the app and can be browsed offline. No account is required.

Setting or sharing a wallpaper creates a temporary local copy on your device. The app does not upload wallpaper files to a developer-operated server. When you share an image, the app or service you select handles it under its own privacy policy.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, phone number, or precise GPS location.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond.

2. Advertising

The app uses Unity LevelPlay to request banner advertisements in wallpaper lists and interstitial advertisements after every third wallpaper opening, when an advertisement is available. Advertisements require an internet connection.

The Android build includes advertising SDKs or adapters for:

  • Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Google AdMob

The networks that serve advertisements depend on the LevelPlay configuration and advertisement availability. Including an adapter does not mean that its network serves every user.

Advertising services may process:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • App interactions, advertisement views, and clicks.
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

These services use information to deliver and measure advertisements, operate their services, and detect fraud. Their collection, sharing, retention, and privacy controls depend on their policies and applicable settings.

3. Your Advertising Choice

When the app starts, it asks whether you agree to personalized advertising processing.

Your choice is passed to LevelPlay as consent values for Unity and Facebook before initialization. The current app does not save this answer between launches, so the question can appear again.

The current app does not use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or explicitly pass this choice through a Google-specific consent API. This dialog does not guarantee that every included advertising network has received or applied your choice.

Choosing “No thanks” does not disable all advertisements or all data processing.

You can manage available advertising ID and advertising privacy controls in Android settings and use the advertising providers’ privacy controls. You can also contact us about your choice or privacy concerns.

5. Permissions and Local Storage

The app uses:

  • Internet access for advertising.
  • Android advertising ID access for advertising services.
  • Permission to set your device wallpaper.

Temporary wallpaper images are stored in app-specific storage. Android may remove temporary files. You can also clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

These actions do not necessarily remove a wallpaper already applied to your device, copies shared elsewhere, or information held by advertising providers.

6. Data Retention and International Processing

Temporary images remain until removed by Android or by clearing the app’s local data.

We retain support emails as needed to address your request and meet applicable obligations.

Advertising providers determine how long they retain information under their own policies. We do not set a single retention period for them. Their processing may take place in countries other than your own.
